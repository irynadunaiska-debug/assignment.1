What is From Home to Home?

From Home to Home is young local business in Cork. We specialize at selling second-hand furniture for homes and offices. Our mission is simple - to help Cork residents organize their space with style, without overpaying, to make sure that every piece of furniture is used to its full potential and protect our environment by doing so.

Why Choose Us?

People donate to us their furniture from all over the Cork, constantly expanding and refreshing our catalog with unique and unforgettable items. We carefully select every item and examine it. After making sure it's in a good condition we upload it to our website with an affordable price.

You Help The Environment and Poeple in Need

By buyng second-hand you reduce the amount of waste in Cork and demand for new production. We send the third of our income to Enable Ireland.

Can You Visit Us In Person?

Yes! We have a showroom right here in the heart of the Cork. Come over and check out our furniture in person at College Rd, University College, Cork.
